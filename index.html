About

My Introduction

I got into cyber security through the technical side — breaking and hardening systems — but what kept me there was the bigger question: how do you translate a technical vulnerability into something a business actually acts on? That's what pulled me toward GRC. Risk only matters if someone with budget understands it.

I'm a Cyber Security MSc student at UEA with 8+ years of programming experience. I've written formal security policy and compliance research, built a CVE-triage tool at CyberHack 2026, hardened a Node.js app against the OWASP Top 10, and modelled IPSec VPNs and ACLs in a Cisco lab — technical depth that lets me assess risk from evidence, not just frameworks.
